## Vision

### 10x Check
Live football that a blind fan can follow on their own phone, in their language, without a human describer. The 10x step beyond the approved plan is not more features. It is reaching all three audiences named in premise 1 with the same verified stream:
- **Blind and partially sighted fans, and fans listening without watching:** Casual and Analyst listening modes.
- **Broadcasters and studios:** recaps, the AD track rendered over a picture, overlays, and MCP for their agents.

The hardest part, verified facts aired in time, stays the core.

## Accepted Scope (added to this plan)
- **Listening modes:** Casual and Analyst replace the detail toggle. Pass distance, accuracy and difficulty, plus ball and shot speed, are defined in the working plan.
- **Recaps:** half-time and full-time, built from code statistics and verified metric claims only, never from cause labels.
- **MCP:** a read-only match-tools server. The Analyst binds to it in-process, every tool is time-bounded, and the truth log is unreachable from it.
- **Broadcast render:** player frames for one render match, with G8 checked on that match, and a rendered AD-track video.
- **Voices:** two per language; screen-reader mode marks explanations with an "Analysis:" prefix.
- **Goal replay:** App voice mode only; it never delays never-drop events.
- **Follow one stat:** press height, xT danger, pass difficulty, shot speed or possession, each with defined thresholds; crossings come from a StatWatcher code executor.
- **Review fixes:**
  - merge and integration time (4 h), and a post-evaluation re-run slot (4 h)
  - a revert-and-re-measure slot for Event Hubs/Cosmos (2 h)
  - Foundry template evaluators for Arabic and Hindi (1.5 h)
  - the `eval-freeze` step at the end of Oct 22, with the Oct 23 run as the run of record (0.5 h)
  - the xT fit and refit (1 h)
  - an Oct 19 milestone check on pre-evaluation hours
- **Design fixes, confirmed at document approval:**

  - xT replaces xG, and a StatWatcher executor produces crossings (CEO-7)
  - hour updates: CEO-1 from 6 to 9 h, CEO-5 from 1 to 1.5 h, CEO-7 from 2 to 3 h
